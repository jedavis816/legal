# Terms of Service

**Last updated:** May 5, 2026

## Service description

This is a personal SMS-based financial automation system ("Mission Control") operated by a single individual (the "Owner") for the Owner's personal use. It provides automated budget status reports, transaction categorization, purchase evaluation, and financial alerts via SMS.

## Eligibility

This service is operated for and used by the Owner exclusively. No other individuals are recipients or users.

## Message frequency and rates

Message frequency varies based on the Owner's commands and scheduled notifications, typically zero to twenty messages per day. Standard SMS message and data rates may apply per the Owner's mobile carrier plan.

## Opt-out

To stop receiving messages, the Owner may reply **STOP** at any time. The system will cease all outbound messaging. To resume, reply **START**.

## Help

For help, reply **HELP** to receive a brief description of available commands.

## Support contact

This system is operated by a private individual. Support is self-administered. For questions about the underlying technology, contact the Owner directly.

## Limitations

This system provides automated financial information and recommendations. It does not constitute professional financial, tax, or legal advice. The Owner is solely responsible for all financial decisions.
